## Full description (up to 4000 characters)

Vassole Smart Player IPTV is a player for your own IPTV sources: M3U/M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts supplied by your provider. Free, no activation and no ads.

IMPORTANT: the app does not include, sell or recommend any channels, movies, series or playlists. You need a playlist or an account from a provider to use it. You are responsible for having the right to access the sources you add.

LIVE TV
• Channels organized by category, with search and favorites
• Program guide (EPG) showing what is on now and next
• Fast channel switching with the remote (CH+/CH− and number keys)
• Channels in several qualities (SD, HD, FHD, 4K): if one fails, the app tries the next

MOVIES AND SERIES
• Catalog with poster, synopsis and rating
• Continue watching where you left off
• Seasons and episodes, with the next episode one click away

LANGUAGES AND SUBTITLES
• Grouping by language and by version (dubbed, subtitled, dual audio)
• Choice of the video's audio track and subtitles
• External .srt and .vtt subtitles with timing, size and color settings

BUILT FOR TV
• Full remote control navigation
• Native player: hardware decoding of HEVC/H.265 and AC-3, depending on the device
• Playback speed, aspect ratio and quality selection

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
• No accounts and no data collection: playlists, favorites and settings stay on your device
• PIN lock for categories and adult content
• Backup and restore of your data to a file

Works on Android phones and tablets, Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Samsung TVs (2018 and later) and LG TVs (2018 and later).
